About Our Department

The Department of Economics at Sri Venkateswara College, University of Delhi, provides an academic environment focused on understanding economic principles, institutions, policies and development.

The department encourages analytical thinking, quantitative skills, research and an understanding of contemporary economic issues.

Academic Areas

Microeconomics

Consumer behaviour, production, firms and market structures.

Macroeconomics

National income, inflation, employment and economic growth.

Development Economics

Poverty, inequality, economic development and public policy.

Financial Economics

Financial institutions, investment and financial markets.

Labour Economics

Employment, wages, workforce and labour markets.

Statistics and Econometrics

Quantitative analysis, statistical methods and economic data.
